Hermes Personal Assistant

A private assistant for one person

Hermes is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps the owner keep up with his own mail and calendar: it reads new mail, sorts it into folders, lists what needs attention, and reminds him of meetings.

It is used only by its owner, with his own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

All the work is done on the owner's computer. Mail and calendar data are not sent to any other service.
